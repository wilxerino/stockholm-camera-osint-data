# Camera dataset license and attribution

The combined camera database in `data/cameras.json` includes OpenStreetMap-derived records. The combined
database is made available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

Data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Trafiken.nu and Fartkameran.se are credited in each record's source links.
The traffic-camera information they attribute to Trafikverket is described
as CC0 by Trafikverket. This repository does not redistribute camera images.

The camera dataset documents published records. It does not certify that
every camera remains present, active, or aimed in a particular direction.
